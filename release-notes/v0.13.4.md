**Satellite ground tracks for the whole list, and a Lock button for decode points**

- With no satellite picked, every satellite in the list (as filtered) now has its ground track on the map:
  - 12 hours back from where it is, drawn faint.
  - 12 hours ahead, dashed.
  - A dot for each hour ahead, numbered 1h to 12h when the map shows 20 satellites or fewer.

  A picked satellite gets the same, always numbered. The tracks are worked out in the background (a few hundred take about a second) and again every 10 minutes.
- The satellite map can now zoom out to the whole world, so Fit shows every listed satellite. At its closest zoom-out before, some were off the edge.
- Spectrum & receiver: a **Lock** button on the toolbar turns decode points' following of their signal (AFC) off and on. Off puts each point back on its own frequency. It's also in Settings ("Lock decode points onto their signal").
- Fix: the list filter read "With fM repeaters (ham)".

Tested in the window test and on generated signals:
- Every listed satellite gets about 12 hours of track either side and 12 hourly dots.
- Fit puts all of them in view.
- Lock off returns points to their own frequency and keeps them there.
