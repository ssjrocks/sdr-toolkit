**Broadcast: every FM station with its RDS, and DAB+ radio**

**FM stations and RDS (new page)**
- Every FM station in the band with its RDS: name, radiotext (what's playing), programme type, traffic flags (TP / TA), alternative frequencies and clock.
- The SDR steps across 87.5-108 MHz: 2 MHz at a time on an RTL-SDR, 16 MHz on a HackRF, every station in view decoded at once; what each station said is kept between sweeps.
- The RDS decoder is the toolkit's own. It fixes short error bursts only while in sync, and needs two blocks in their right places before it trusts a sync, so noise doesn't turn into made-up names.

**RDS in Spectrum & receiver**
- In WFM, the station you're tuned to shows its name and radiotext in the status line and the decoded log.

**DAB+ digital radio (new page)**
- Every station of the ensembles on the channels watched: a region's (Australia's 9A/9B/9C and regional blocks, the UK's), all of band III, or your own list.
- What's playing (DLS), programme type and audio format; the station's slideshow picture in Details.
- **Listen to ...** plays the station picked in the table; the SDR then stays on its channel.
- Decoded by welle.io (`welle-cli`), played through ffmpeg; the installer adds both.

**Also**
- Details can show a picture from this PC (the DAB+ slides).
- Windows: the whole-band FM engine and the DAB engine are allowed in the helper.

Tested:
- **RDS:** generated stations through the page and the receiver; noise gives nothing. **Off air, indoors, on the HackRF:** 101FM ("It's all about the music!"), KIIS973 and triple j decoded. The RTL-SDR's small antenna hears FM too weakly for RDS here.
- **DAB+:** a two-station ensemble generated with OpenDigitalRadio's encoder, multiplexer and modulator (stations, now-playing text, slides, listening). Live, no DAB+ ensemble reaches this PC indoors, so it hasn't decoded a real one yet.
