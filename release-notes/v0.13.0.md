**Satellite telemetry, and right-click to listen to a satellite**

- Right-click a satellite in the satellite list or in Coming over for:
  - **Listen for its beacon**: a CW beacon decoded as Morse, or another beacon.
  - **Listen for its telemetry**: decoded, see below.
  - **Listen to its repeater / transponder output**: an FM repeater, or the middle of a linear transponder in SSB.

  Each entry names the downlink it will use ("437.800 FM voice") or says the satellite has none. Spectrum & receiver then tunes there and follows the Doppler shift through the pass.
- New decoder: **Satellite telemetry (gr-satellites)**. It gets a 40 kHz IQ channel that follows the Doppler shift.
  - gr-satellites knows about 380 satellites by NORAD number; for those it shows the telemetry fields.
  - Other satellites are decoded from SatNOGS' description of the downlink (FSK, GFSK or GMSK up to 19200 baud), which works for the many that send plain AX.25. Packets show as "From callsign to callsign: text".
  - Each packet's full dump is in Raw data.
  - Linux only: the installer adds Ubuntu's gr-satellites package; there's no Windows version.
- Fix: dropdowns in the settings down the right side opened as wide as their longest entry, past the edge of the window and onto the next monitor. They now open at the box's own width, with long entries wrapped.

Tested:
- 1200 baud AX.25 telemetry in a generated recording, 300 kHz off the dongle's centre, decoded through the receiver's IQ into one line per packet.
- The right-click menu on the ISS, and that its telemetry entry starts the decoder for NORAD 25544 with Doppler following.
- A settings dropdown opens no wider than its box (checked offscreen, not on your two-monitor Wayland desktop).

Not yet tried on a real satellite pass, or on a satellite with a telemetry layout gr-satellites knows.
