**Tables stay where you put them; typing a frequency isn't interrupted**

- Every table's columns can now be resized and dragged into another order: live tables (ADS-B, AIS, radiosondes, ISM...), decoder tables, the satellite lists, the DMR / P25 explorer's lists, the logbook and the relay list.
  - Columns start sized to their contents and only widen as wider values come, so they don't twitch.
  - A column you size yourself is left alone.
  - Each mode's live table remembers your column order, widths and sort between sessions.
- New data no longer moves a table:
  - The view stays where you scrolled it.
  - A selected aircraft, ship or sonde stays selected without the table jumping to it on every update.
  - A decoder's table follows new rows only while you're at the bottom of an unsorted table, as a log does.
- Only your own picks move a table: clicking a plane, ship or sonde on the map selects its row and scrolls to it.
- Typing a frequency into Spectrum & receiver's frequency box is no longer overwritten while you type. Doppler following retuned every second and replaced what you'd typed. The box now waits until you press Enter or leave it; entering your own frequency then stops the Doppler following.

Tested in the window test:
- A 300-row ADS-B table rewritten in reverse order keeps its scroll position and selection; a map click on that aircraft then scrolls to its row.
- A column sized by hand keeps its width when longer values arrive.
- A moved column is still moved after switching to AIS and back, and AIS keeps its own layout.
- Text typed in the frequency box survives a retune.
