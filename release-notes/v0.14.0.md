**DMR / P25: every call saved and listed, networks kept apart, and picking a row that stays picked**

- **Calls tab.** The DMR / P25 explorer saves every call as its own WAV and lists it in a new **Calls** tab in the right sidebar.
  - The list shows time, talkgroup, radio, length, network and frequency.
  - Filter it by network, or search for a talkgroup, radio ID or frequency.
  - Click a call for its details at the bottom of the sidebar, with **Play**, **Show in folder** and **Copy details**. Double-click to play it.
  - Calls go to `Music/SDR Toolkit calls`, a folder per frequency. Change the folder or turn saving off in Settings. Calls saved before are listed when the page opens.
- **Networks kept apart.** When several systems are heard at once, each channel now shows its network: a DMR colour code ("DMR · colour code 1"), a P25 system ("P25 phase 1 · WACN BEE00 SYSID 658") or a NAC. An **All networks** box above the lists shows one network's channels, systems, talkgroups and radios at a time. Each call keeps the network it was on when it was saved.
- **Fix: picking a row in the explorer's lists.** The lists re-sort as calls come in. The selection could jump to another radio or channel just before you clicked, so "Only the selected" picked the wrong one.
  - Buttons now act on the item you selected, wherever it has moved.
  - A list doesn't change while your mouse is over it; it catches up when the mouse leaves.
  - The same applies to the ADS-B / AIS / sonde tables and the satellite lists.
- DSD-FME now runs in a folder of its own, so saving calls doesn't leave a stray `WAV` folder where the app was started.

Tested:
- On a generated DMR recording, the explorer saves the call as a WAV in its frequency folder.
- Call files are read correctly (talkgroup, radio, length, network), including after a restart.
- In the window test, the Calls tab lists, filters and shows a call's details.
- "Only the selected" takes the picked radio after four re-sorts. This test fails on 0.13.5's code.
