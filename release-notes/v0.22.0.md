**One self-contained app: the decoders come in their own bundle; satellites, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, drones and cellular pages**

**The tools bundle (Linux)**
- Every outside decoder the pages run now comes in one bundle in the app's own folder (`~/.local/share/sdr-toolkit/tools`), with the libraries it needs: rtl_433, readsb, direwolf, WSJT-X's jt9, multimon-ng, the RTL-SDR tools, GNSS-SDR, gr-satellites and GNU Radio's modules, gr-lora_sdr, the radiosonde decoders, rtlamr, wmbusmeters, the aircraft-message decoders, welle.io, SatDump, ffmpeg.
- Nothing of it is installed system-wide. Each program finds its own libraries through a small wrapper, so no other program on the PC sees them, and removing the app removes them.
- The installer, and the app's Install button, download the bundle (checked by its SHA-256) instead of installing Ubuntu packages. The only Ubuntu packages left are the app's own libraries, the SDRs' device permissions, tshark (Wi-Fi monitor mode) and compilers for DSD-FME.
- DSD-FME / mbelib stays a build on your PC, after its patent notice, as before.
- The bundle is built in an Ubuntu container of the same release (`scripts/build-tools-bundle.sh`), so building it doesn't install anything either.

**New pages**
- **Weather satellites and Inmarsat (SatDump):** METEOR-M2-3 / M2-4 pictures, recorded by themselves on every pass over you (from the tracker's elements), into the logbook and Details; Inmarsat STD-C and AERO messages.
- **Hydrogen line (21 cm):** the galaxy's hydrogen against radial velocity, from a long average; narrow interference filtered out.
- **Meteor scatter:** pings from a distant transmitter's carrier, with their length, strength and Doppler.
- **Bluetooth LE devices and drone Remote ID:** every device, with its maker; trackers marked (Apple Find My away from or near its owner, Samsung SmartTags, Tiles); drones' Remote ID (ID, position, height, speed, and the pilot's position) on the map.
- **Wi-Fi networks and devices:** monitor mode on an interface of its own (your normal Wi-Fi is left as it is): networks, their devices, the networks devices look for, channel use; your own network decrypted with its password; Open in Wireshark. If Wi-Fi is switched off, it says so instead of turning it on.
- **Zigbee and Thread devices (HackRF):** the toolkit's own 802.15.4 receiver: networks, devices with their makers, frame kinds.
- **Analog FPV video (HackRF):** the picture from 5.8 GHz FPV transmitters.
- **LTE cells:** every tower cell in the bands scanned, with its channel, band, physical cell ID, FDD or TDD, and level.

**Also**
- Makers from the IEEE register (Wi-Fi, Zigbee, Bluetooth public addresses).
- Details can show a picture from this PC; a page can put pictures into the logbook.
- SDR Tools Relay 0.3.4's fix in the built-in relay: quitting can no longer crash it.

Tested:
- **Bundle:** built in a container, installed here, every program's libraries resolved, the decoders started; the full test suite run against it with nothing skipped for want of a decoder.
- **LTE:** cells found live indoors on a HackRF in bands 28, 5, 8, 3, 1, 7 and 40.
- **Bluetooth LE:** live here (phones, a Find My device).
- **SatDump, hydrogen line, meteor scatter, Wi-Fi, Zigbee, FPV:** on generated or recorded signals only; the HackRF ran them live without errors, but nothing to decode was heard (indoors, Wi-Fi switched off).
