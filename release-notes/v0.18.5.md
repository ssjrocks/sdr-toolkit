**Separate settings for each kind of SDR**

The SDR pages' settings were one set for every radio, so a setting made while using a HackRF stayed for the RTL-SDR. Some RTL-SDR settings do nothing on a HackRF (direct sampling, the RTL-SDR's gain), so changing them there showed no effect, and they broke the RTL-SDR afterwards. Direct sampling left on puts an RTL-SDR in its HF mode, and above 30 MHz it then hears nothing.

- **Settings now follow the SDR in use.** The Settings tab says whose they are ("Settings for this PC's RTL-SDR", "Settings for this PC's HackRF", or a relay's), and switches when the SDR does.
- **A HackRF's page doesn't show the RTL-SDR's own settings** (device, gain, RTL AGC, direct sampling, offset tuning, bias tee): its gain, amp, antenna power and filter are on its bar.
- **The sample rate and frequency correction are kept apart** for the RTL-SDR and the HackRF. An RTL-SDR's page offers only the rates it can deliver, so 19.2 MS/s chosen for a HackRF never reaches it.
- **Settings you made before are kept as the RTL-SDR's.** If your RTL-SDR still hears nothing, check its page's Settings for **Direct sampling** (Off, except below 24 MHz on a V3) and a manual gain set very low.
