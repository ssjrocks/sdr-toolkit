**Fixes: the explorer hanging, dropping samples and losing the dongle**

- Stopping a DMR / P25 decoder took 2 s, because DSD-FME didn't notice the stop while it waited for audio. It now ends at once. This was behind several problems:
  - The receiver froze for 2 s every time a decode point was removed. The explorer removes one whenever a signal turns out not to be digital voice, so the receiver fell behind and showed thousands of "blocks dropped (too slow)".
  - Stop ended every decoder one after another. With 20 running, the window hung long enough for Ubuntu to ask whether to close it.
  - Meanwhile Stop could crash ("dictionary changed size during iteration"). The relay or dongle connection then stayed open and the decoders kept running. Starting again gave "the rtl_tcp server closed the connection (the dongle may be in use)".
- Decoders are now stopped off the receiver's thread, and all at once when you press Stop. Stop always lets go of the relay or dongle, even if something else goes wrong.
- The explorer missed strong signals. A nearby repeater's skirts stay above the noise for tens of kHz, so it was taken for something wide (a broadcast) and only its loose edges were tried. Wide regions are now measured again 25 dB below their peak, where DMR is about 10 kHz wide and a broadcast is still wide. The same fix helps Radiosondes with a strong sonde nearby.
- The explorer now tries a signal only once it shows up in two looks in a row (2 s apart), so a noise peak or a click doesn't start a decoder.

Tested: 8 DMR decode points on a recording. Removing four doesn't hold up the receiver, and Stop takes under 3 s with no DSD-FME left running. Before the fix, the same test hung and crashed the same way as on the relay. The explorer finds a strong generated DMR signal every time, and not its side bands or noise. Not yet tried live through the relay.
