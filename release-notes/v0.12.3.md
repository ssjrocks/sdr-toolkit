**Fix: this PC's own RTL-SDR never opened, and Stop froze the window**

- With the dongle plugged into this PC (rather than through a relay), Spectrum & receiver, the explorer and the other modes that use rtl_tcp gave up after 10 s. The error showed rtl_tcp's help text about OsmoSDR. The app waited for rtl_tcp to print "listening...", but Ubuntu's rtl_tcp (2.0.2) keeps that line in its output buffer until it exits. The app now connects as soon as rtl_tcp's port answers, about 0.6 s.
- Stop (and switching modes) froze the window for 3 s with this PC's dongle. Once a client has connected, rtl_tcp 2.0.2 catches the stop signal but never exits. It was being killed after 3 s; now it's killed after half a second.

Tested on this PC's RTL-SDR (R828D), twice back to back. It opened in 0.6 s, received the full 2.4 MS/s with no blocks dropped, and stopped in 0.5 s, and the dongle opened again at once. Also tested with a stand-in rtl_tcp that buffers its output like Ubuntu's: the old code failed after 10 s, the new code connects at once.
