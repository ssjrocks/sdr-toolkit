**More decoded: every ISM band, one row per device, signals nothing decodes, meters, every digital voice protocol**

**ISM sensors (rtl_433), rebuilt:**
- **Every band.** The default listens to 315, 345, 433.92, 868 and 902-928 MHz; region choices (Australia / NZ, North America, Europe) narrow that down. Until now it only ever heard 433.92 MHz, so anything on 915-928 MHz in Australia was never heard.
- **Whole bands at once on a HackRF.** Each band is taken whole (up to 17 MHz) and cut into 1.2 MHz channels, each decoded by an rtl_433 of its own, so nothing is missed while hopping elsewhere in the band. An RTL-SDR hops between the bands.
- **One row per device.** What it is, its latest readings, its frequency, how many times heard, how often it sends, its signal, and first and last heard. Details shows every field and the range each reading has covered. Every message still goes to the decoded log, and a new device is noted when first heard.
- **Signals nothing decodes.** rtl_433's pulse analyser looks at every burst. A repeating signal no decoder names is listed as Unknown, with its modulation, timings and the decoder spec that would read it. **Add as a decoder** turns it into one of yours, decoded by name from then on.

**Utility meters (new page):**
- ERT meters (gas, water, electricity: SCM, SCM+, IDM, NetIDM, R900) through rtlamr.
- Wireless M-Bus meters and sensors (T1/C1, S1) through rtl_wmbus and wmbusmeters, which picks the right driver for each meter itself.
- The installer builds rtlamr and installs wmbusmeters. It also turns off wmbusmeters' own background service, which would otherwise watch for SDRs.

**DMR / P25 / digital voice:**
- **The explorer tries every protocol.** A found signal gets DSD-FME's automatic detection first, then NXDN48, NXDN96, dPMR, M17, P25 phase 2, EDACS and ProVoice in turn; until now it gave up after automatic. "Try signals as" picks one protocol instead.
- **Trunk following** on the one-frequency page: point it at a control channel, and DSD-FME follows each call onto its voice channel and back. A channel map can be given for DMR Tier III, Capacity+, Connect+ and EDACS, along with a talkgroup to hold and whether to follow private calls. Also new there: X2-TDMA, EDACS EA and the trunked P25/DMR mode.

Tested:
- **Whole-band ISM:** live on a HackRF, hopping between 433 MHz (2 channels) and 915-928 MHz (13 channels at once), and on generated signals (a sensor 2.9 MHz off-centre decoded at the right frequency).
- **Analyser to decoder:** a generated remote no decoder knows was listed as Unknown, added as a decoder, then decoded.
- **Meters:** decoding tested on sample messages. The ERT pipeline ran live on an RTL-SDR, but no meter was heard, so ERT decoding hasn't been proven on a real meter.
- **Trunk following:** not yet tried on a live trunked system.
