**Logbook: the pictures and calls received, to see and play again**

- The logbook has a new **Recordings & pictures** tab. It lists every file received:
  - SSTV and weather-fax pictures, from the radio (Spectrum & receiver, satellites) or heard through the speakers (a WebSDR).
  - Every DMR / P25 call the explorer recorded, the whole calls library.

  Each entry has its time, mode, description and where it came from (the frequency and network for a call). Pick one for a preview of the picture, or Play for a call, plus **Show in folder** and **Open**; double-click to play or open. The mode filter, search and Export CSV work on it as on the other tabs.
- Pictures are the ones SSTV and weather fax save (their Save settings, in `Pictures/SSTV` and `Pictures/WEFAX`), so there's still one copy of each. Pictures already in those folders from before are listed too, by their file names; nothing else in the folders is taken.
- Calls saved before are listed when the DMR / P25 page next opens. A copy replaced by a better recording of the same transmission comes off the list.
- Clearing the logbook takes them off the list; the files themselves stay.

Tested in the window test:
- A decoded SSTV picture is listed with "heard on" its source.
- An older picture in the folder is listed with the time from its name, and an unrelated file beside it is not.
- The explorer's recorded calls are listed.
- The tab previews a picture and offers Play for a call.
