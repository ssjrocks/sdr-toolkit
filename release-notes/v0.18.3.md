**Fix, from your report: DMR / P25 decoding almost nothing at 19.2 MS/s**

At a HackRF's 19.2 MS/s, the receiver needed 1.1 seconds of processing for every second of signal (measured with four DMR channels). It all ran on one core, so it fell behind. The live stream doesn't wait, so blocks were thrown away, and DSD-FME got audio full of gaps: the run of P25 CRC errors in your log. Two changes:

- **The receiver's own demodulator is skipped when nobody hears it.** In the DMR/P25 explorer its audio is muted and the voice comes from the decode channels, yet it was demodulating every sample: half a core at 19.2 MS/s. It now runs only while its audio is played, decoded or recorded. The signal meter takes its reading from the spectrum meanwhile.
- **The decode channels have a thread of their own**, so their work runs alongside the receiver's on another core.

Tested on a 19.2 MS/s DMR recording streamed in real time through a relay, with four DMR channels:
- Before: 659 blocks dropped in 15 seconds.
- Now: none, and the DMR decodes.

If the decode channels ever do fall behind, the status line now says so ("decode channels behind: N blocks lost") instead of losing audio silently.
