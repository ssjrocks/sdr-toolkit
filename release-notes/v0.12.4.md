**Fixes: clicking the spectrum tuned somewhere else, and the explorer sat beside signals**

- A click on the spectrum or waterfall tuned to the nearest **Tuning step** (100 kHz by default). Clicking a signal at 401.837 MHz tuned 401.8. A click now tunes to the nearest kHz (10 Hz in USB, LSB and CW); the step is only for the arrows and the wheel.
- Clicking near the left or right edge of the band, or on the dongle's centre spike, retuned the dongle so the clicked signal sat a quarter of the band right of centre: the whole picture jumped. The dongle now moves only as far as needed, so the signal stays about where you clicked.
- A click on the grab bar along the top that moved a pixel or two slid the band. It now takes a drag of 4 px.
- DMR / P25 explorer: a signal found was given a decode point on the nearest 6.25 kHz channel. With a transmitter or dongle a few kHz off, that was beside the signal. The point now goes where the signal is (to 100 Hz). A signal found again within 1.5 kHz of a known channel keeps that channel's frequency.

If signals look a few kHz off their usual channels, check the ppm correction in the RTL-SDR settings.

Tested: a click tunes to the kHz clicked with a 100 kHz step; a click in the edge moves the dongle by under 150 kHz (it was 1.75 MHz); a twitchy click on the bar doesn't slide; the explorer puts its point within 1 kHz of a DMR signal 3.4 kHz off the raster. Not yet tried live.
