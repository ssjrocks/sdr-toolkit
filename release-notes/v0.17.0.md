**Any SDR: HackRF over a relay, satellites at every frequency, link meters**

**A HackRF on a relay** (SDR Tools Relay 0.2.0): every SDR mode uses it as it would an RTL-SDR.
- Tuning goes to 6 GHz. Spectrum & receiver also offers the HackRF's 4.8, 9.6 and 19.2 MS/s.
- Modes that want less than the HackRF's 2 MS/s get it narrowed down here (filtered, then decimated), so ADS-B, AIS, LoRa and the rest work unchanged.
- **HackRF…** next to Start sets the amp, antenna power, manual LNA / VGA gain and the baseband filter. A running receiver takes them at once; other modes start again with them. They're kept for next time.

**Satellites at every frequency:** the tracker no longer hides or marks as "out of range" what an RTL-SDR can't tune. S-band and higher downlinks are listed and can be tuned. Each one says what it takes to hear it: a HackRF or similar SDR, usually a dish, and above 6 GHz a downconverter. The satellite filters ("With SSTV pictures" and so on) count them too.

**Link meters:** the SDR pages show two bars at the top:
- **USB link:** what the device is delivering (this PC's dongle, or the relay's RTL-SDR or HackRF).
- **Relay network link:** what's arriving over the network, the ping, and the buffers the relay had to drop.

Each bar fills against what the sample rate needs: green keeping up, orange falling short, red dropping. The relay's figures need SDR Tools Relay 0.2.0.

**The list is reorganised:**
- The speaker-audio modes (Text, Digital, Pictures, Packet & paging, Tones) are under **WebSDR**.
- "RTL-SDR" is now **SDR**.
- **Listen to** (the sound device) is on each WebSDR page, rather than above every page.

**Fix:** if a mode couldn't start again after a settings or relay change (a missing recording, a refused relay), the window carried on as if it were running and logged decoder errors. It now stops and shows why.

Tested against a stand-in relay and a stand-in HackRF library; not yet with a real HackRF.
