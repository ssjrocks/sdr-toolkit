**DMR / P25 explorer**

The DMR / P25 page is now an explorer for mapping digital voice in your area. It has the receiver's spectrum and waterfall: slide to a busy part of the band and leave it running.

- Every signal that appears is tried with DSD-FME for about 20 s. Digital voice (DMR, P25, NXDN, D-Star, YSF, dPMR, M17) is kept and listed. Anything else is let go for 10 minutes. A strong signal's side bands aren't mistaken for signals of their own.
- Lists fill as things are heard. **Channels** shows what each one is in plain words, plus its colour code or NAC, calls, who's talking now and what its data carried. **Systems** groups channels that belong together. **Talkgroups** and **Radios** show every ID heard, how they connect and a radio's last GPS position. **Activity** logs each call, position and text message as it comes.
- Listen to every channel at once, or single out one channel, talkgroup or radio. The others stay muted while their calls are on.
- The lists are kept between sessions, so exploring builds a picture of the area.
- The old single-frequency DMR / P25 decoder is still in Spectrum & receiver, under **Decode with**.

Tested on generated DMR bursts in a recording, alongside a plain carrier and noise. The DMR signal is found, kept and listed; the carrier is let go; no noise or side band is taken for a signal. The lists, listening choices and saving were tested with DSD-FME's output lines. Not yet tried on a live band: busy real-world bands, trunked systems and P25 haven't been checked.
