**LoRa finder: LoRa decoded without knowing its settings**

The LoRa page decodes one exact setup: a frequency, spreading factor, bandwidth and sync word. Anything else is invisible to it. The new **LoRa finder** page works the settings out for every burst in view:

- **Finds the bursts** in the 2.2 MHz around its centre frequency and measures each one's width from its spectrum. LoRa fills 62.5, 125, 250 or 500 kHz evenly. Narrower bursts are listed as "not LoRa" (meters, sensors and alarms using FSK or OOK), once a minute per frequency.
- **Works out the spreading factor** (7 to 12) by de-chirping: only the right spreading factor turns each LoRa symbol into a single tone. Inverted chirps are recognised too; LoRaWAN downlinks use them.
- **Finds the sync word and decodes** with gr-lora_sdr, trying 0x34 (LoRaWAN), 0x2B (Meshtastic) and 0x12 (MeshCore and private LoRa). It remembers which one worked on each channel.
- **Reads what it can:**
  - LoRaWAN: the kind of message, device address (The Things Network's recognised), frame counter, port and payload size, and a join request's device and join EUIs. The payload itself is encrypted with the network's keys.
  - Meshtastic: texts and positions on the default channel.
  - MeshCore: the Public channel and adverts.

Each burst is a row: time, frequency, what it is, what it says, the settings found, width, length and signal strength. The default centre, 917.4 MHz, covers Australia's usual LoRaWAN uplink channels. The How to tune tab lists where Meshtastic, MeshCore and LoRaWAN downlinks are, here and elsewhere.

Tested on a generated band recording holding five bursts. Each was found, named and read with the right settings:
- a Meshtastic text: SF11, 250 kHz, sync 0x2B;
- a MeshCore Public message: SF7, 62.5 kHz, 0x12;
- a LoRaWAN uplink from a The Things Network address: SF9, 125 kHz, 0x34;
- an inverted LoRaWAN downlink acknowledging it: SF10, 500 kHz;
- an FSK burst, listed as not LoRa.

Not yet tried live: real bursts can be weaker, overlap, or use settings it doesn't try.
