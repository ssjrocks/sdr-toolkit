**DMR / P25 in plain words, and what the data carried**

- DMR and P25 lines now say what a signal is: "DMR voice channel, colour code 1, slot 1 and slot 2", "P25 phase 1 control channel, NAC 654", "DMR repeater, idle", "DMR data channel". Each comes with a short note on what that means ("A trunked system's control channel: the calls are on other frequencies"). This is in Spectrum & receiver, decode points and the DMR / P25 page. Identify names DMR results the same way.
- Calls show as "Group call to 505 from radio 3125678, slot 1", marked encrypted or emergency when they are, once per call rather than every superframe.
- What the data frames carried is shown instead of a bare count: GPS positions from location reports (LRRP), text messages, data types and control messages (channel grants, network status, Capacity Plus...). Summaries break it down too, e.g. "decoded: 12 GPS positions, 2 text messages, 40 control messages", and say when DSD-FME couldn't decode the data further.
- Fix: Identify's rtl_433 (ISM devices) trial never ran (a clash of settings).
- Fix: Radiosondes showed the last updates of a recording only after the next one came.

Tested: the new descriptions on DSD-FME output lines in the formats its source prints, and on generated DMR bursts. Not yet tried on live P25 or trunked DMR.
