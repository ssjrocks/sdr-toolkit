**Aviation, marine and emergency: aircraft messages, VOR / ILS, marine DSC, 406 MHz distress beacons**

**Aircraft messages (new page): ACARS, VDL Mode 2 and HFDL**
- ACARS (129-132 MHz) through acarsdec, VDL Mode 2 (136 MHz) through dumpvdl2, HFDL (2-22 MHz) through dumphfdl, with libacars decoding what's inside (ADS-C positions, CPDLC, flight plans). The installer builds all four.
- Several channels at once from the SDR's raw samples; one row per aircraft with its latest message, and positions on the map.
- A strong aircraft is also decoded on the channels next to its own; the same message now counts once, at the loudest channel.
- **Joined to ADS-B:** an aircraft's Details tab in ADS-B lists the ACARS / VDL2 / HFDL messages it sent, matched by ICAO address or registration.
- HFDL on an RTL-SDR: through the tuner (V4, upconverter) or direct sampling (Blog V3); a HackRF tunes there itself.

**VOR and ILS beacons (new page)**
- Every VOR and ILS channel in view at once (2 MHz on an RTL-SDR, up to 8 MHz on a HackRF).
- A VOR's radial (the bearing from the station you're on) and the bearing to it.
- An ILS localizer's left / right and a glide slope's up / down deviation, as DDM and as a share of full scale; Details gives a localizer's paired glide slope.
- Each beacon's Morse ident, read by the toolkit's Morse decoder.
- ATIS is voice: listen on AM in Spectrum & receiver.

**Marine DSC, VHF channel 70 (new page)**
- The toolkit's own ITU-R M.493 decoder: distress alerts (MMSI, what's wrong, position, time), their acknowledgements and relays, urgency and safety calls, and routine calls (who calls whom and on which channel).
- One row per station; distress positions on the map.

**406 MHz distress beacons (new page): EPIRBs, PLBs and ELTs**
- First-generation COSPAS-SARSAT beacons (nearly all in use), decoded by the toolkit: country, protocol, MMSI / aircraft address / serial, 15-hex ID and GPS position, checked by both BCH codes. Frame decoding follows dec406 (F4MLV, MIT licence).
- Self-tests are marked; any other burst is flagged as a possible distress.
- Second-generation (spread-spectrum) beacons aren't decoded yet.

**Also**
- Faster channel cutting (spectrum decode points, whole-band ISM, sondes, VOR): all channels in one batched transform, in single precision. 160 channels at 9.6 MS/s now take half the time real time allows.
- Windows: the whole-band ISM engine and the LoRa finder couldn't start (the helper didn't allow them); fixed, with a test that every engine is allowed.
- `SDR_TOOLKIT_SILENT=1` keeps all sound off the speakers (DSD-FME's voice too); the tests set it.

Tested:
- **ACARS:** blocks built to ARINC 618 and modulated as acarsdec's demodulator expects, decoded through the page (two aircraft, one twice). A real aircraft burst in a HackRF capture here synced but had 4 parity errors (acarsdec fixes up to 3), so ACARS hasn't decoded a real aircraft here yet.
- **VDL2 and HFDL:** the decoders run with the page's command lines on recordings; parsing tested on JSON shaped as their sources write it. Neither has decoded a real signal yet.
- **VOR / ILS:** synthesized to ICAO Annex 10, radials within 0.5° on four bearings, DDM within 0.002, idents decoded. Live on a HackRF (108.9-117.1 MHz, 163 channels), no beacon was heard from indoors.
- **DSC:** calls built to ITU-R M.493, decoded down to about 7 dB signal-to-noise. 10 minutes live on channel 70 with an RTL-SDR: no calls heard.
- **406 MHz:** dec406's published example frames decode with both BCH codes passing; synthesized bursts decode at the right frequency. 90 s live on a HackRF ran cleanly; no beacon was heard.

Not done yet: FLARM / OGN, second-generation 406 MHz beacons.
