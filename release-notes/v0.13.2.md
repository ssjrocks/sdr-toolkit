**Satellite tracker: pick on the map, list by what they carry, no waiting on every visit**

- **Pick on the map.** Clicking a satellite on the map picks it as if you'd chosen it in the list: it's selected (and scrolled to) on the left, and the tabs below show its passes, radios, sky plot and details. The tab you have open stays open, so with Radio open you see that satellite's radios. Clicking an empty part of the map lets it go: the selection and its tabs clear, and the map shows every listed satellite that's up again.
- **List by what they carry.** The list's dropdown now also has these, under the kinds:
  - With FM repeaters (ham)
  - With linear transponders (SSB)
  - With SSTV pictures
  - With APRS / packet
  - With CW beacons
  - With telemetry you can decode
  - With weather pictures

  Each one counts only downlinks that are still heard.
- **Faster to come back to.** The satellite list and orbits were already downloaded only when due (orbits every 12 hours, the satellite and radio lists every week). But every visit to the page read the whole 6 MB list again and worked out every satellite's next passes again. Both are now kept between visits: the list for an hour, the passes for an hour while your location and minimum elevation stay the same. The status line now says "downloading" only when it actually is.
- Fix: changing the list's filter could quietly switch the selected satellite to whichever one landed in its row.

Tested:
- A map click picks a satellite (Radio tab open) and a click on empty map clears it.
- With FM repeaters lists the ISS and SAUDISAT 1C; with SSTV lists only the ISS.
- A second visit gets the list and passes at once without working them out; a different location works them out again.
